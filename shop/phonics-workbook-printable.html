Help your child learn to read, one sound at a time! The Poppy & Bo Phonics Starter Workbook is a printable, 41-page, full-color workbook for ages 4-6 that takes little learners from letter sounds to reading their first words and sentences. Four friendly guides talk to your child on every page. Includes an ink-saver black-and-white copy of every page, too.

WHAT'S INSIDE (41 pages, full color, plus an ink-saver black-and-white copy)

  • Letter sounds: all 26 letters with a picture, the sound and a word.
  • First sounds: say the picture and circle the letter it starts with.
  • Short vowels: a, e, i, o and u, with tracing and pictures.
  • Word families: -at, -an, -ig, -og, -ug, -en, -un, -ed, -ap and -ot, with a sentence to read on every page.
  • Sound it out: touch each sound dot and blend three-letter words.
  • Match the word: match pictures to words.
  • Rhymes: circle the picture or word that rhymes.
  • Read and color, and read and draw: first sentences.
  • Start pages: cover, "This workbook belongs to", meet the friends and how to use.
  • Rewards: a Phonics Ready checklist, a Reward Sticker Sheet and a Phonics Star certificate.

Fun, Informative And Easy To Follow

  • Poppy, Bo, Fern and Pebble guide every page with a speech bubble.
  • A short "Did you know?" note and a skills label on every page, so parents and teachers can see what it teaches.
  • Tick-off boxes on each page to celebrate every step.

How To Use

Print at 100% on plain paper or cardstock, or open the PDF on a tablet and write with a finger or stylus in any PDF or drawing app. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is plenty. Say the SOUND of each letter, not its name. Use the ink-saver pages for everyday home printing and keep the full-color pages for your tablet.
