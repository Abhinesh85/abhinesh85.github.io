How To Use

Print at 100% on plain paper or cardstock, or open the PDF on a tablet and trace with a finger or stylus in any PDF or drawing app. Dashed grey letters, numbers and shapes are for tracing. Pictures and hollow shapes are for coloring. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is plenty for ages 3-5. Use the ink-saver black-and-white pages for everyday home printing, and keep the full-color pages for your tablet or special pages. Tip: slide pages into a clear sleeve and use dry-erase crayons to reuse them again and again.
