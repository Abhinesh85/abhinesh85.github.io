Make math friendly and fun! The Poppy & Bo Math Starter Workbook is a printable, 38-page, full-color workbook for ages 4-6 that takes little learners from counting to adding, taking away and number bonds to 10. Four friendly guides talk to your child on every page. Includes an ink-saver black-and-white copy of every page, too.

WHAT'S INSIDE (38 pages, full color, plus an ink-saver black-and-white copy)

  • Counting: count and write 1-10, count and match, and number tracing 1-10.
  • Ten frames: fill the frames and write the number.
  • Comparing: more or fewer, which is bigger (> < =), number order, one more and one less.
  • Adding: add to 5, add to 10 and more adding, with pictures to count.
  • Taking away: take away from 5 and from 10 by crossing out.
  • Number bonds to 5 and to 10, plus patterns and odd-one-out puzzles.
  • Start pages: cover, "This workbook belongs to", meet the friends and how to use.
  • Rewards: a Math Ready checklist, a Reward Sticker Sheet and a Math Star certificate.

Fun, Informative And Easy To Follow

  • Poppy, Bo, Fern and Pebble guide every page with a speech bubble.
  • A short "Did you know?" note and a skills label on every page, so parents and teachers can see what it teaches.
  • Tick-off boxes on each page to celebrate every step.
