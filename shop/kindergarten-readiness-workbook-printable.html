Get your child ready for kindergarten with fun, friendly practice! The Poppy & Bo Kindergarten Readiness Workbook is a printable, 34-page, full-color workbook for ages 4-6. Four friendly guides talk to your child on every page, every page has a fun fact and a skills label, and big bold dashed lines are easy for small hands to trace. Includes an ink-saver black-and-white copy of every page, too.

WHAT'S INSIDE (34 pages, full color, plus an ink-saver black-and-white copy)

  • Pre-writing (7 pages): straight lines, zigzags and waves, curves and loops, shape tracing, writing your own name, pencil-control mazes and scissor skills.
  • Sorting and patterns (5 pages): big and small, same and different, odd one out, sort it out and what comes next.
  • Counting (5 pages): count and match 1-5 and 6-10, more or fewer, count and write, and number order to 20.
  • Position words (3 pages): left and right, top, middle and bottom, inside and outside.
  • Letters (3 pages): find the letter, first sounds and big and small letters.
  • All about me (3 pages): all about me, my family and how do I feel.
  • Start pages: cover, "This workbook belongs to", meet the friends and how to use.
  • Rewards: a Kindergarten Ready Checklist, a Reward Sticker Sheet and a Kindergarten Ready certificate.

Fun, Informative And Easy To Follow

  • Poppy, Bo, Fern and Pebble guide every page with a speech bubble.
  • A short, true "Did you know?" fun fact on every page.
  • A small skills label on each page so parents and teachers can see what it teaches.
  • Three tick-off boxes on each page to celebrate every step.

How To Use

Print at 100% on plain paper or cardstock, or open the PDF on a tablet and trace with a finger or stylus in any PDF or drawing app. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is plenty. Use the ink-saver pages for everyday home printing and keep the full-color pages for your tablet. Tip: slide pages into a clear sleeve and use dry-erase crayons to reuse them again and again.
