Make December learning merry! The Poppy & Bo Christmas Learning Pack is a printable, 24-page, full-color workbook of holiday counting, patterns, tracing and matching for ages 3-6. Four friendly guides talk to your child on every page, and gifts, stars, cookies and snowflakes make practice feel like play. Includes an ink-saver black-and-white copy of every page, too.

WHAT'S INSIDE (24 pages, full color, plus an ink-saver black-and-white copy)

  • Tracing (4 pages): straight lines, zigzags and waves, curves and loops, and shape tracing.
  • Counting (6 pages): count and write 1-10, count and match 1-5 and 6-10, more or fewer, and number order to 20.
  • Patterns and sorting (4 pages): what comes next, odd one out, big and small, same and different.
  • Letters and words (2 pages): Christmas words with first letters, and find the letter.
  • Feelings (1 page): how do I feel.
  • Start pages: cover, "This pack belongs to", meet the friends and how to use.
  • Rewards: a Reward Sticker Sheet and a Christmas Star certificate.

Fun, Informative And Easy To Follow

  • Poppy, Bo, Fern and Pebble guide every page with a speech bubble.
  • A short "Did you know?" note and a skills label on every page.
  • Three tick-off boxes on each page to celebrate every step.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE (3 files) 1. Full-color PDF, US Letter (8.5 x 11 in), 24 pages, with clickable bookmarks 2. PNG pages (ZIP, 250 dpi, one image per page) 3. ZIP with the full-color A4 PDF plus the ink-saver black-and-white PDFs (US Letter and A4), for everyday printing

How To Use

Print at 100% on plain paper or cardstock, or open the PDF on a tablet and trace with a finger or stylus in any PDF or drawing app. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is plenty. Use the ink-saver pages for everyday home printing and keep the full-color pages for your tablet.
