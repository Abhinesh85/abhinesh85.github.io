Make learning letters, numbers, shapes and colors fun! The Poppy & Bo Preschool Learning Pack is a printable, 72-page, full-color workbook for ages 3-5. Four friendly guides talk to your child on every page, each page has a fun fact and a skills label, and big bold dashed lines are easy for small hands to trace. Includes an ink-saver black-and-white version of every page, too. A great way to build pre-writing skills, counting and letter recognition at home, in the car, on a rainy day or with a homeschool group.

WHAT'S INSIDE (72 pages, in full color, plus an ink-saver black-and-white version of every page)

  • Alphabet A-Z (26 pages): a big colored letter to look at, a dashed letter to trace, practice rows, a silly "say it" rhyme, the letter sound (for example, "B says /b/ as in ball") and 2-3 pictured words with the first sound highlighted.
  • Numbers 1-20 (15 pages): the numeral, the number word, tick circles and a counting set to count and color. Counting rhymes on pages 1-10.
  • Shapes (9 pages): circle, square, triangle, rectangle, oval, star, heart and diamond. Each page shows how many sides and corners it has and real-world examples (a plate is a circle!). Plus a Shape Hunt scene.
  • Colors (8 pages): red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink and brown. Name real objects of each color and learn which colors mix to make new ones.
  • Days of the Week and Months of the Year (2 pages).
  • Bonus pages: pencil-control paths, scissor-cutting lines and shapes, and a "My Family" drawing page.
  • Start pages: cover, "This pack belongs to", Meet the Friends, How to Use, and an Activity Ideas guide for parents and teachers.
  • Rewards: Progress Chart, Reward Sticker Sheet and a Super Learner Certificate.

How To Use

Print at 100% on plain paper or cardstock, or open the PDF on a tablet and trace with a finger or stylus in any PDF or drawing app. Dashed grey letters, numbers and shapes are for tracing. Pictures and hollow shapes are for coloring. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is plenty for ages 3-5. Use the ink-saver black-and-white pages for everyday home printing, and keep the full-color pages for your tablet or special pages. Tip: slide pages into a clear sleeve and use dry-erase crayons to reuse them again and again.
