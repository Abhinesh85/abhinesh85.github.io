Help your child learn to read with fun, friendly practice! The Poppy & Bo Sight Words Workbook is a printable, 56-page, full-color workbook that teaches 40 pre-primer sight words (the Dolch pre-primer list) for ages 4-6. Four friendly guides talk to your child on every page, and each word gets its own page with four steps: say it, trace it, find it and write it, then read it in a sentence. Includes an ink-saver black-and-white copy of every page, too.

WHAT'S INSIDE (56 pages, full color, plus an ink-saver black-and-white copy)

  • 40 word pages: a, and, away, big, blue, can, come, down, find, for, funny, go, help, here, I, in, is, it, jump, little, look, make, me, my, not, one, play, red, run, said, see, the, three, to, two, up, we, where, yellow, you.
  • Each word page: a big colored word, a dashed word to trace, a circle-the-word game, writing lines and a simple sentence to read.
  • 8 review pages: four Word Check pages and four Find the Words games, one set after every 10 words.
  • Start pages: cover, "This workbook belongs to", meet the friends and how to use.
  • Rewards: a My Sight Words checklist, a Reward Sticker Sheet and a Sight Word Star certificate.

Fun, Informative And Easy To Follow

  • Poppy, Bo, Fern and Pebble guide every page with a speech bubble.
  • A short "Did you know?" note and a skills label on every page, so parents and teachers can see what it teaches.
  • Three tick-off boxes on each page to celebrate every step.

How To Use

Print at 100% on plain paper or cardstock, or open the PDF on a tablet and trace with a finger or stylus in any PDF or drawing app. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is plenty. Use the ink-saver pages for everyday home printing and keep the full-color pages for your tablet.
