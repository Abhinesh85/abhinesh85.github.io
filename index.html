    Good to know

    Shipping, returns & FAQ

    How long does an order take?

    Every piece is made to order. We'll message you if anything about your timeline changes. Need it by a date? Say so in the personalization box or message us before you order.

    How much is shipping?

    Shipping is by USPS to anywhere in the US and depends on what you order, shown in your cart before you pay. Made-to-order items totaling $75 or more ship free. Digital downloads never ship.

    Can I personalize my order?

    Yes. Choose "Add name" or type your text in the personalization box on the product page. For logos, photos or a layout of your own, request a quote or message us.

    Returns & problems with my order

    We want you to love your order, and we'll work with you to make it right. Please message us before sending anything back so we can understand what happened, find the best fix together and approve your return. If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, send us a photo within 14 days and we'll gladly replace it or refund you, and we'll cover the return shipping.

    Because each piece is personalized just for you, we can't accept returns for a change of mind. If we do approve a return for another reason, the return shipping is paid by the buyer. Digital downloads are final sale, but if a file won't open, we'll help you right away.

    Can I cancel my order?

    Yes, message us as soon as you can. We can cancel for free until production starts, usually within 24 hours of ordering. After that the piece is already being made.

    How do I care for my tumbler?

    Hand wash for the longest-lasting print. UV prints and engraving won't peel or wash off with normal use, but avoid abrasive scrubbing and soaking for long periods.

    Do you offer bulk or business orders?

    Yes: weddings, teams, schools, corporate gifts and memorials. Tap "Request a quote" above with your quantity and date and we'll reply within a day.

    Where is my order?

    Use Track my order with your order number and the email you used at checkout.
