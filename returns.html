Returns & refunds

Last updated November 1, 2026

The short version: personalized items are non-refundable except for mistakes or defects. If something is wrong with your order, we will make it right.

If there is a mistake or defect

If your order arrives damaged, has a defect, or is not what you ordered (including a printing error on our side), send us a photo within 14 days of delivery. We will replace it or refund you, your choice, and we will cover the return shipping if we ask for the item back.

Please message us before sending anything back so we can understand what happened and approve the return.

What we cannot accept

  • Returns for a change of mind, because each piece is made just for you.
  • Spelling, dates or pictures that match what you typed or uploaded at checkout. Please check these before paying.
  • Normal color differences between a screen and the printed item.

If we agree to take back an item for another reason, return shipping is paid by the buyer.

Canceling an order

Message us as soon as you can. We cancel for free until production starts, usually within 24 hours of ordering. After that the piece is already being made.

Digital downloads

Downloads are final sale. If a file will not open or is not what the listing described, tell us and we will help you right away.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take about 5 to 10 business days to show it.

How to reach us

Use the chat on our shop or email hello@prajocustomstudio.com with your order number and, for a problem, a photo.
