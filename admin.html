Tumbler designs

How to add a design
  1. Tap ＋ Add designs and choose your flat wrap artwork (the image you print on a tumbler). You can pick several at once.
  2. Give each one a clear name. Customers see it in the gallery and on the tumbler page.
  3. Drag the ⠿ handle to change the order. The first ones show first. Use the switch to hide a design without deleting it.

Best artwork: a wide image (around 2000 × 1700 px), JPG or PNG. Transparent PNGs are placed on white. We resize everything automatically.

Each product has its own designs. Pick the product first, then add or sort its designs.

How to update your site

Everyday jobs (no coding)

Add or change a product
On this page, tap + New listing (or tap a product). Add photos, price, stock and description, then Save. It appears on the store straight away.
Add a product people can design (mug, coaster, badge…)
⚙ Settings → 🧩 Products to design → ＋ Add a product. Name it, choose Flat or Round, upload a straight-on photo of the blank product, then drag the gold dots to mark where the artwork prints. It becomes a button on the “Design yours” page.
Add a design to the gallery
⚙ Settings → 🥤 Tumbler designs → ＋ Add designs. Upload the flat artwork and name it. It shows up in the 3D gallery and the tumbler designer. Drag to reorder, switch off to hide.
Change words on the website
⚙ Settings → Site settings. Headline, intro, banner, shipping and returns answers (FAQ), contact details and more.
Pause orders while you're away
Turn on 🌴 Vacation mode in the Settings menu. Digital downloads stay available.
Shipping prices and local pickup
⚙ Settings → Checkout options.
See and answer customers
Open the pricing app → Messages. You also get a Telegram alert with a link.
Look at orders
Open the pricing app → Orders. Move each order along: New → In production → Ready → Shipped → Completed.

Good to know

  • Changes you make here show on the live site within a minute. Refresh the store page to see them.
  • Pictures are resized for you, so don't worry about file size. JPG and PNG work best.
  • Sign-in uses your password plus the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (and your backup app). Keep both safe.
  • Hidden things are never deleted: switch a design or product off to hide it, and back on to bring it back.

Things that need a developer (or Claude)

  • Changing the page layout, adding new kinds of sections, or new features.
  • Swapping the base tumbler photo used in the designer (the file tumbler-white.jpg).
  • Updating the website files on GitHub.

Everything else on this page you can do yourself.
