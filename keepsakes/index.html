Printed in gold. Cut in Sacramento.

Every keepsake starts as dark cast acrylic. We print your design straight onto it with a UV printer, then a laser cuts the shape and the ring hole from the same starting corner, so print and cut line up.

Material
3 mm dark cast acrylic. Laser cutting works cleanly on dark, opaque cast sheet, so that is what we use.
Print
UV-cured gold and colour ink, printed directly on the acrylic.
Ring hole
4 mm, with at least 3 mm of acrylic around it.

Questions.

The short answers. For anything else, write to hello@prajocustomstudio.com.

What is the gold, exactly?

Printed gold ink, drawn in layers to look like foil. It is not gold leaf or real foil.

Why only dark acrylic?

Our laser cuts dark, opaque cast acrylic cleanly. White, clear and blue sheets do not cut well with it, so we do not offer them.

How are the flower, stone and star chosen?

By month for flowers and stones, and by date for the zodiac. Enter a birthday in the collection and you see all three together.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Add one to four JPG, PNG or WebP photos, then drag each one on the preview to frame it. Photos stay in your browser until you choose to send them.

Can I see it before it is made?

Yes. The last step gives you a picture of your proof and a request file. You send both to the shop and we confirm price and availability before anything is made.
