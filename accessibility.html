Accessibility statement

Last updated November 1, 2026

We want everyone to be able to browse and order from PrAjo Custom Studio, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control or magnification.

What we do

  • We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.
  • Pictures have text descriptions, forms have labels, and the site works with a keyboard. A "Skip to content" link is at the top of the home page.
  • Text and buttons are sized to be readable and tappable on phones.
  • Moving effects, such as holiday snow and the flipping design previews, stop automatically if your device is set to reduce motion. You can also stop all motion yourself with the Pause animations button at the bottom of every page.

Known limits

Our design tools show previews of your picture on a product. These previews are visual, and we describe the chosen design by name to help screen-reader users. Some third-party pages, such as Stripe checkout, follow their own accessibility standards.

Tell us about a problem

If something on our site is hard for you to use, email hello@prajocustomstudio.com and tell us the page and what happened. We reply within 2 business days and will fix it or help you place your order another way, for example by chat or email.
