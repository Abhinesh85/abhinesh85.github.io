Privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

PrAjo Custom Studio is a small made-to-order business in Sacramento, California. This page explains what information we collect when you use prajocustomstudio.com, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Orders. Your name, email, phone number and delivery address, entered on the secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe handles your card details; we never see or store your card number.
  • Messages and quote requests. The name, email, message and any photos or logos you send us through the chat, the Bulk & team orders form or the design tools.
  • Designs you create. The names, text and pictures you enter or upload in our design tools so we can print them.
  • On your own device. Your cart, saved favorites, chat session and settings are kept in your browser's storage on your phone or computer. They are not sent to us until you place an order or send a message.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on the store, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

The store shows how many people are on it right now. For that, your browser shares a random code for the current visit and which page you are on. Our server also works out an approximate place (city and ZIP area) from your internet connection using a location service (ipwho.is), so the shop owner can see where visitors come from. We do not keep your IP address, the place is only held for a few minutes while you are on the site, and it is not shared with anyone else. It is switched off if your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

How we use it

  • To make, ship and deliver your order, and to send order updates.
  • To answer your questions and quote requests.
  • To keep the site safe, for example to stop spam and abuse.
  • To keep records we need for taxes and accounting.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the shop, and only what they need:

  • Stripe: payments and checkout.
  • Supabase: our secure database and private file storage for orders, messages and uploaded pictures.
  • Telegram: private alerts to our team when an order or message arrives.
  • Cloudflare: forwards email sent to our hello@ address.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • USPS and local delivery: your name and address, to deliver your order.
  • Etsy: some product photos load from Etsy's image servers, so your device's IP address is visible to Etsy when a page loads. All fonts are served from our own site.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: as long as needed for tax and accounting rules, usually up to 7 years.
  • Messages and uploaded pictures: as long as needed to finish your order and help you afterwards. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@prajocustomstudio.com from the address you used with us and say what you would like. We reply within 45 days. We may need to keep order records we are required to keep by law. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

To delete your data: email hello@prajocustomstudio.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your order number if you have one.

Do Not Track and tracking by others

Our store does not track you across other websites, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, so we respond to a "Do Not Track" signal the same way for everyone: nothing is tracked. No third party collects information about your browsing on our store for advertising. Fonts from Google and some product photos from Etsy load from those companies' servers, which can see your IP address when they do.

California privacy rights

  • Notice at collection: the information listed under "What we collect" is collected to take and deliver your order, answer you, and keep our records. We keep it for the time given under "How long we keep it."
  • No sale or sharing: we do not sell or share personal information with anyone for advertising, and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.
  • Shine the Light: we do not disclose personal information of California residents to third parties for their own direct marketing.
  • No penalty for asking: we treat you the same whether or not you use these rights. To use them, or to appoint someone to ask for you, email hello@prajocustomstudio.com.

Children

Our shop is for adults and parents. Our printables are for children to use, but they are bought by parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Pages are served over HTTPS, payments go through Stripe, uploaded pictures are stored privately, and only the shop owner can open orders and messages. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care with what you share.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Questions? Email hello@prajocustomstudio.com.
