Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

PrAjo Custom Studio is a small made-to-order business in Sacramento, California. This page explains what information we collect when you use prajocustomstudio.com, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Orders. Your name, email, phone number and delivery address, entered on the secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe handles your card details; we never see or store your card number.
  • Messages and quote requests. The name, email, message and any photos or logos you send us through the chat, the Bulk & team orders form or the design tools.
  • Designs you create. The names, text and pictures you enter or upload in our design tools so we can print them.
  • On your own device. Your cart, saved favorites, chat session and settings are kept in your browser's storage on your phone or computer. Unless you turn the visit counter off (see below), a copy of what is in your cart is also saved on our server so we can see unfinished carts. Other things stay on your device until you place an order or send a message.
  • Visit counter. A random code kept in your browser, plus which page or product you looked at, when you added something to your cart, started checkout or placed an order, your device type (phone, tablet or computer), your time zone, the website or link that sent you to us, how long you stayed on the page and how many pages you saw, and an approximate place (country, region, city and ZIP area). The place is worked out from your internet connection by a small program on our own Cloudflare account, which does not keep your IP address, and it is only as exact as your internet provider's area, often not your street. The visit counter has no name, email or IP address. We use it to see how many people visit, where they come from and which products interest them. Visit records are deleted after 400 days.
  • Cart reminder email. Only if you choose to type your email into the cart box, we keep it with your cart and may email you about that cart.
  • Emails you send us. When you email hello@, orders@, support@ or proofs@prajocustomstudio.com, we keep the message, your address and our replies in our inbox system so we can help you.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not share the visit counter with any advertiser or analytics company. The visit counter is our own: it is stored only in our own database.

The store also shows how many people are on it right now. For that, your browser shares a random code for the current visit and which page you are on. Our server also works out an approximate place (city and ZIP area) from your internet connection using a location service (ipwho.is), so the shop owner can see where visitors come from. We do not keep your IP address, the place is only held for a few minutes while you are on the site, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Turning the counter off. The visit counter and the saved copy of your cart are switched off automatically if your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal. You can also use the Your privacy choices link at the bottom of any page to turn them off or on. If you visit from the European Union, the United Kingdom or a similar region, they stay off until you say yes.

How we use it

  • To make, ship and deliver your order, and to send order updates.
  • To answer your questions and quote requests.
  • To see how many people visit and which products they look at, so we can improve the shop.
  • To send the cart reminder you asked for, and to reply to your emails.
  • To keep the site safe, for example to stop spam and abuse.
  • To keep records we need for taxes and accounting.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the shop, and only what they need:

  • Stripe: payments and checkout.
  • Supabase: our secure database and private file storage for orders, messages, emails, the visit counter, saved carts and uploaded pictures.
  • Resend: sends the emails we write to you (order updates, replies and cart reminders).
  • Telegram: private alerts to our team when an order, message or email arrives. An alert can include the sender, subject and the first lines of a message.
  • Cloudflare and Gmail: receive and forward email sent to our store addresses.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • USPS and local delivery: your name and address, to deliver your order.
  • Etsy: some product photos load from Etsy's image servers, so your device's IP address is visible to Etsy when a page loads. All fonts are served from our own site.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: as long as needed for tax and accounting rules, usually up to 7 years.
  • Messages, emails and uploaded pictures: as long as needed to finish your order and help you afterwards. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time. Emails we mark as spam are deleted after 90 days.
  • Visit counter data: up to 400 days, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Saved carts, and the email on them: until you check out or empty your cart, and no longer than 90 days after you last changed it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@prajocustomstudio.com from the address you used with us and say what you would like. We reply within 45 days. We may need to keep order records we are required to keep by law. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

To delete your data: email hello@prajocustomstudio.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your order number if you have one.

Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and tracking by others

We respond to both "Do Not Track" and "Global Privacy Control" signals from your browser by switching off the visit counter and the saved copy of your cart for that browser. Our store does not track you across other websites. No third party collects information about your browsing on our store for advertising. Fonts from Google and some product photos from Etsy load from those companies' servers, which can see your IP address when they do.

California privacy rights

  • Notice at collection: the information listed under "What we collect" is collected to take and deliver your order, answer you, and keep our records. We keep it for the time given under "How long we keep it."
  • No sale or sharing: we do not sell or share personal information with anyone for advertising, and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.
  • Shine the Light: we do not disclose personal information of California residents to third parties for their own direct marketing.
  • No penalty for asking: we treat you the same whether or not you use these rights. To use them, or to appoint someone to ask for you, email hello@prajocustomstudio.com.

Visitors outside the United States

We are in California and keep your information on servers in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar region, we use your information only to run the shop (to make and deliver your order, to answer you, and to meet legal duties), and we ask your permission before using the visit counter or the saved cart copy. You can withdraw that permission at any time with the Your privacy choices link. You may ask to see, correct, delete or move your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing us, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our shop is for adults and parents. Our printables are for children to use, but they are bought by parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Pages are served over HTTPS, payments go through Stripe, uploaded pictures are stored privately, and only the shop owner can open orders and messages. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care with what you share.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Questions, or a request about your data? Email hello@prajocustomstudio.com.
